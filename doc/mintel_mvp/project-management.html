Project Management Plan

This page defines governance, timeline, deliverables, ownership, and risk controls for delivering MinTel MVP in a structured and auditable way.

Workstreams

Workstream Owner Output
Data & ETTL Data Engineering Reliable ingestion, features, retraining jobs
Product AI ML Engineering Bundle generation and pricing logic
Customer AI Data Science Profiles, segments, behavior models
Simulation & Recommendation Applied AI Outcome forecasts and targeting
Web Application Full-Stack Team Operator dashboard and analytics UI

Delivery Milestones

  • M1 - Foundation: Data contracts, schema, baseline ETL, environment setup.
  • M2 - Core Models: Initial product generation, pricing model, customer segmentation.
  • M3 - Intelligent Matching: Recommendation service + simulation loop integrated.
  • M4 - Dashboard MVP: Decision cockpit with KPI monitoring and drill-down pages.
  • M5 - Validation: Scenario testing, model quality checks, stakeholder sign-off.

Governance

  • Weekly steering meeting
  • Daily engineering standup
  • Bi-weekly model review board
  • Change management log for assumptions

Key Risks

  • Data drift and feature instability
  • High simulation compute cost
  • Slow model retraining pipelines
  • Low adoption by business users

Mitigation Controls

  • Data quality gates and anomaly alerts
  • Model monitoring with rollback strategy
  • Batch + near-real-time serving design
  • UX walkthroughs with marketing team
