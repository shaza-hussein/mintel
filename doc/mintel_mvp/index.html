MinTel: Centralized Decision Engine for Product Optimization and Retention

MinTel is an AI-powered telecom platform that simulates, analyzes, and optimizes core operations. It combines generative product design, dynamic pricing, customer intelligence, recommendations, and simulation to increase revenue and improve retention.

Purpose

Enable faster and smarter telecom decisions with minimal manual effort by integrating data, model learning, and scenario simulation in one system.

Problem 1: Product Inertia

Traditional workflows are manual and slow. Delays in bundle design and pricing lead to missed market opportunities and weaker competitive response.

Problem 2: Weak Targeting

Rule-based targeting oversimplifies customer behavior, reducing campaign relevance and return on marketing spend.

System Scope

MinTel addresses the full loop from product ideation to launch simulation and customer matching.

  • Product Layer: Generate bundles with GenAI, optimize pricing, and forecast expected adoption, activity, and revenue.
  • Customer Layer: Build rich behavioral profiles and segment-level intelligence for hyper-personalized strategy.
  • Recommendation Engine: Match generated products to the most relevant customer groups.
  • Simulation Engine: Run controlled scenarios before launch to estimate business outcomes and risk.
  • Data & Infrastructure Layer: Operate automated ETTL pipelines to keep data and models current.

Complexity Highlights

High Compute Large Data Automation-Ready
  • Daily ingestion at tens of millions of records
  • Feature store at hundreds of millions of rows
  • Retraining cadence from hourly to weekly
  • GPU training for deep models and generation
